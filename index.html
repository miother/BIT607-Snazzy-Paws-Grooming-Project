Welcome to Snazzy Paws Grooming

Snazzy Paws Grooming, based in the heart of Hamilton, offers a gentle, one-on-one grooming experience tailored to your dog’s unique needs. From essential care to luxury spa packages, every session is delivered with expert skill, patience and attentive care to ensure your pet looks and feels their absolute best.

Meet the owners

Snazzy Paws Grooming was founded by a local Hamilton couple whose shared passion for dogs inspired them to create a warm, professional space for pet care. Both owners work full-time in the salon alongside their dedicated team to ensure every dog receives attentive, high-quality care. As active, modern pet lovers themselves, they are committed to providing a seamless, stress-free experience for local owners and their furry family members.
